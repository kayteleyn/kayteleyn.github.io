Reptile Care

General Care Advice

Reptiles like bearded dragons, leopard geckos, and turtles require specialized care, and small details in their environment can greatly impact their health. Their diet varies by species—some reptiles eat insects, others eat leafy greens, and some require a mix of both. It’s important to research the specific needs of your reptile and provide a balanced diet that supports their growth and energy levels. Insect‑eating reptiles should be fed gut‑loaded insects like crickets or dubia roaches, while herbivorous species need a variety of fresh greens. Commercial reptile foods can be used as supplements, but whole foods should make up the majority of their diet. Brands like Repashy and Zoo Med offer high‑quality options.

Reptiles rely heavily on their environment to regulate their body temperature, so providing proper heating, lighting, and humidity is essential. A secure terrarium with appropriate substrate, hides, and climbing areas helps them feel safe and comfortable. UVB lighting is crucial for many species, as it supports bone health and prevents metabolic disorders. Maintaining the correct temperature gradient allows reptiles to move between warm and cool areas as needed. Regular cleaning of the enclosure helps prevent bacterial growth and keeps your reptile healthy.

Reptiles can be sensitive to stress, so handling should be gentle and consistent. Pay attention to their behavior, as changes in appetite, activity level, or shedding can indicate health issues. Regular vet checkups are important, especially for species prone to metabolic bone disease or respiratory infections. With proper care and attention to their environmental needs, reptiles can live long, healthy lives and become fascinating companions.

Food Reccomendations

Some high‑quality reptile food brands to consider include:

  • Repashy Grub Pie or Veggie Burger
  • Zoo Med Natural Bearded Dragon Food
  • Fluker’s High‑Calcium Cricket Diet

Reptile diets vary by species, but insect‑eaters should be fed gut‑loaded crickets, dubia roaches, or mealworms. Herbivorous reptiles need leafy greens like collard greens, mustard greens, and dandelion greens. Calcium and vitamin D3 supplements are essential for preventing bone issues. Avoid feeding insects caught outdoors or foods like spinach and iceberg lettuce.
