House Cat Care

General Care Advice

Care for cats is more important than one would think, and small things that seem minor can significantly increase your cats life quality and health. What you feed your cat can be very beneficial, and is best to feed your cats food suitible for their life stages. For newborn kittens, letting them drink their mothers milk and/or giving them kitten milk replacement. Never feed a kitten cows milk, being how it can give them severe digestive issues. When a cat reaches junior/adult/elder years, making sure they have clean water constantly and feeding them high quality kibble/wet food for their age is optimal. On the packaging of these foods, the age range should be included, so be sure to buy for the right age range. It's also important not to swap their food too often, due to how it can cause issues such as digestive issues, vomiting, and diarrhea. It's also reccomended to attempt to find your cats favorite flavor of food, so try testing different flavors of foods such as salmon, chicken, shrimp, and other meats to see what they like the most. Canned food for cats is reccomended, supporting kidney health rather than simple kibble.

As for caring for indoor cats, regularly cleaning out their litter is crucial. Many cats would rather do their business on your carpet rather than a full litterbox. Not cleaing it can also result in urinary problems, from cats holding in their urine due to not wanting to use their full litter box. The droppings within these litterboxes can also release harmful bacterias, both in the air and on the cat whenever they use their litter box. Parasites are also a risk, infecting humans, which can be fatal, especially if the owner is pregnant, risking complications. If you have multiple cats, it's a rule of thumb to have one litter box per cat. Having multiple litter boxes enables the cats to use them more peacefully and its easier with cleanup, being how the boxes get full less frequently.

People think that cats are low maintanence-- they are-- however they still require mental stinumation. Regularly playing with your cat is great for their mental and physical health, so invest in some cat toys, inckluding some that the cat can play with whenever you're not home. To help the health of your cats nails, also invest in a scratching post, keeping their claws sharp and out of your furniture.
