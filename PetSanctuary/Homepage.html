Pet Sanctuary

Hello and Welcome to my website Pet Sanctuary! My goal here is to teach new pet owners how to care for their pets. My site goes into general care, and even provides food reccomendations! The animals included on my website, as you can see in the navigation menu, are cats, dogs, bunnies, guinea pigs, hampsters, fish, birds, and reptiles. My hope for this website is that less animals are unintentionally neglected, and handled with care to make sure they live a long, happy life.
