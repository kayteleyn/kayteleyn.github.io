Pet Sanctuary

Hello and Welcome to my website Pet Sanctuary! My goal here is to teach new pet owners how to care for their pets. My site goes into general care, and even provides food recommendations! The animals included on my website, as you can see in the navigation menu, are cats, dogs, bunnies, guinea pigs, hamsters, fish, birds, and reptiles. My hope for this website is that fewer animals are unintentionally neglected, and instead handled with care so they can live long, happy lives.

Website Recommendations

Within my pet care pages, I recommend toys and specific food brands. To buy these for your pet(s), I recommend shopping on Chewy, a site dedicated to pets with toys, food, furniture, and many more things your pet will love!
