Bird Care (parakeets and cockatiels)

General Care Advice

Caring for small birds like parakeets and cockatiels requires attention to their diet, environment, and social needs. Their diet should include a balanced mix of pellets, seeds, and fresh vegetables. Pellets should make up the majority of their diet, as seed‑only diets can lead to nutritional deficiencies. Fresh vegetables like leafy greens, carrots, and broccoli can be offered daily, while fruits should be given sparingly. Avoid toxic foods such as avocado, chocolate, and caffeine. Brands like Harrison’s, Roudybush, and ZuPreem are commonly recommended for pellets.

Birds need a spacious cage with horizontal bars for climbing and plenty of perches of different textures to support foot health. Toys that encourage chewing, shredding, and problem‑solving help keep them mentally stimulated. Birds also need daily out‑of‑cage time to stretch their wings and explore safely. Keeping their environment clean is essential, as dirty cages can lead to respiratory issues. Regularly changing cage liners, washing food and water dishes, and cleaning perches helps maintain their health.

Birds are social animals that thrive on interaction, whether with their human caregivers or other birds. Gentle handling, talking to them, and offering enrichment activities help build trust and prevent boredom. Birds can hide signs of illness, so monitoring their behavior, droppings, and appetite is important. Regular vet checkups and proper grooming, such as nail trimming and occasional baths or misting, help keep them healthy. With consistent care, birds can live long, fulfilling lives and become affectionate companions.
